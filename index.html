About Us

ABHAR Multi-Specialty Hospital is committed to providing quality healthcare services with compassion, integrity, and advanced medical expertise.

Healthcare Driven by Care & Trust

Run by Dr. Anil Mor and his team, ABHAR Multi-Specialty Hospital offers specialized healthcare services across multiple departments with a patient-centric approach. Our hospital is dedicated to delivering safe, ethical, and affordable medical care.

Located at Rajiv Chowk, Thana Road, Meham, Haryana, our facility is equipped to handle emergency care, consultations, diagnostics, pharmacy services, and specialized treatments.
